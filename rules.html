{Rules}

💬

Communication

We use Discord for communication and announcements.

⚠️ If you have questions regarding tasks or infrastructure, send them via the ticket system on our Discord server. Do not post task-related questions in public channels.

🌍

Regional Leagues

The competition is held in independent regional leagues, each with their own scoreboard. The main event takes place in Algeria, but participants from all regions are welcome to join.

01

Team Registration

Team registration must be done by the team captain only, who must verify the team's status via an email address or applicable document.

02

One Team Per Player

Each player may only participate in one team. No multi-team participation allowed.

03

Team Size

The number of players in a single team is not limited for the online competition.

04

Shared Account

You need to share one team account to access the competition platform. Your captain will receive login details before the competition starts.

05

No Attacking Infrastructure

Competitors are not allowed to attack other teams or the competition infrastructure.

06

No Automated Scanners

The use of automated scanners or tools that would overwhelm the infrastructure is prohibited.

07

Flag Format

The flag format is CyberLymph{fl4g_h3r3}, unless explicitly specified in the challenge description.

08

No Sharing

Sharing flags, hints, solutions, or any challenge-related information between different teams or in public sources is strictly prohibited.

09

Respect

Participants must remain respectful and refrain from using inappropriate language on the competition platform or in official communication channels.

10

Organizer Rights

Organizers may extend or modify the ruleset at any time. Organizers reserve the right to temporarily ban or disqualify any team suspected of breaking the rules.
